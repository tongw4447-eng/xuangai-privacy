Xuan Gai Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Overview

Xuan Gai is an entertainment app featuring daily fortune messages, playful divination, blessing effects and regional folklore.

Information we use

The app may request device location permission to determine a country or region for regional themes. Location is used on the device to select content and is not sent to our servers. If permission is unavailable, the app uses the device language and region settings instead.

Virtual Fuyuan balances and local activity records are stored on the device. The app does not require an account and does not collect names, contacts, passwords or payment card details.

Sharing

The app does not sell personal information or share location data for advertising. If you choose to use your device's share function, the content is sent to the app you select.

Purchases

Any future digital purchases must be processed by Google Play Billing. Virtual Fuyuan has no cash value and cannot be withdrawn.

Children

The app is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

Local records remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact tongw4447@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with material revisions.
